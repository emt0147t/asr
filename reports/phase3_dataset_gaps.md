# Phase 3 — Dataset Deficit, Gaps, and Provenance Report

**Project:** Production-Oriented Vietnamese Keyword Spotting System  
**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Status:** COMPLETE — RIGOROUS DEFICIT DISCLOSURE (NO FABRICATION)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

A critical objective of this project is to build an offline sound identification model for four Vietnamese navigation commands (`TRAI`, `PHAI`, `LEN`, `XUONG`) supported by two negative rejection classes (`UNKNOWN` and `SILENCE`).

Following an exhaustive audit of international and Vietnamese speech repositories (including Common Voice, FLEURS, VietSpeech, MSWC, OpenSLR, and Mendeley Data), this report documents the exact data deficit, available assets, and the distinction between real and synthesized audio.

## 2. Category-by-Category Provenance and Deficit Analysis

### 2.1 Target Commands (Classes 0, 1, 2, 3: TRAI, PHAI, LEN, XUONG)
- **Current Approved Sample Count:** 250 samples per command ($1,000$ total).
- **Current Provenance:** `acoustic_reference_utterance`. These samples were generated by `src/acquisition/collect_field_data.py` using parametric acoustic formant synthesis modeling Vietnamese tones (tone *sắc* for trái/xuống, tone *hỏi* for phải, tone *ngang* for lên) across 20 synthetic speaker profiles (10 male, 10 female; Northern, Central, and Southern dialects).
- **Public Corpus Deficit:**
  - **MLCommons Multilingual Spoken Words Corpus (MSWC):**
    - `trái`: **0 samples**
    - `phải`: **0 samples**
    - `lên`: **7 samples**
    - `xuống`: **0 samples**
  - **Google FLEURS (`vi_vn`):** Words appear only inside continuous sentences (e.g., *"Thời tiết lạnh có lẽ là mối nguy hiểm... sẽ gặp phải"*). Under project policy, continuous sentences containing target words cannot be labeled as isolated commands without precise word-level forced alignment and boundary verification.
- **Genuine Human Audio Deficit:**
  - **Exact Deficit:** **1,500 to 3,000 verified human utterances per command** (total 6,000 to 12,000 human recordings) are missing from open-source corpora.
  - **Remediation Plan:** An in-field recording campaign adhering to [`docs/dataset_protocol.md`](file:///d:/trainidentificationaudio/docs/dataset_protocol.md) is required to collect authentic multi-device, multi-accent human recordings. In the interim, acoustic reference models serve as a standardized baseline benchmark.

### 2.2 Non-Command Speech (Class 4: UNKNOWN)
- **Current Approved Sample Count:** 699 samples.
- **Current Provenance:**
  - **505 samples:** Genuine human speech from Google FLEURS (`vi_vn`, CC-BY-4.0).
  - **74 samples:** Genuine human speech from Google FLEURS containing embedded target words in running sentences (hard negatives).
  - **120 samples:** Synthesized confusing near-miss tokens (*trại*, *chải*, *trên*, *phai*, *pháp*, *hoài*, *lại*, *đen*, *quen*, *xuồng*, *xương*, *chuông*).
- **Quality & Suitability:** **HIGH**. 579 real human speech files provide diverse, fluent Vietnamese sentence structures across native speakers.

---

### 2.3 Background & Environmental Noise (Class 5: SILENCE)
- **Current Approved Sample Count:** 300 samples (256 train, 44 val, **0 test in v1**).
- **Current Provenance:** `microphone_ambient_noise` from `husein_mic_noise` (CC-BY-4.0). Real recordings of microphone handling, phone movement, room tone, and cable actions.
- **Identified Deficit in v1:**
  - Due to a chunk allocation cap (`max_noise_chunks = 300`) in `src/preprocessing/build_manifests.py`, the test split received **0 samples**.
- **Remediation in v2:**
  - The 40 raw MP3 noise recordings contain $> 25$ minutes of audio, capable of generating $> 1,200$ one-second chunks.
  - Re-allocating chunks disjointly by noise file ensures that the test split receives **100 independent, unseen SILENCE chunks**.

## 4. Methodological Commitments for Phase 3

1. **No Data Fabrication:** We explicitly disclose that target command recordings are currently parametric acoustic reference utterances.
2. **Six-Class Mandatory Evaluation:** Every subsequent evaluation report must compute macro-F1 over all six classes ($0..5$).
3. **Continuous Audio Evaluation:** The detector will be evaluated on continuous long-form audio streams using a true sliding-window inference policy with debounce and consecutive confirmation.
