## 3. Governance Boundaries & Non-Generalization Mandate

1. **Synthetic Audio Boundary:** All evaluations were performed on synthetic speech generated by VieNeu-TTS v3turbo. Under no circumstances may these results be reported as validation on natural human speech.
2. **Fixed Voice Inventory:** The approved TTS inventory comprises 25 total voices. Evaluated test clips use held-out voices from the v8/v9 training splits, but these voices appeared in historical campaigns. Generalization is established for **unseen text prompts on a fixed synthetic voice inventory**, not arbitrary unencountered human speakers.
3. **Open-Mic Separation:** Success in push-to-talk bounded capture must **never** be cited to claim readiness for always-listening open-mic applications.
