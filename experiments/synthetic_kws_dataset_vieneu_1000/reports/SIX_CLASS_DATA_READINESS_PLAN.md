## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Formulation

The current VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo dataset consists strictly of four canonical Vietnamese command classes:
$$\mathcal{C}_4 = \{\texttt{TRAI}, \texttt{PHAI}, \texttt{LEN}, \texttt{XUONG}\}$$
While this 4-class configuration allows isolated phonetic and acoustic validation of directional keywords, deploying a practical keyword spotting (KWS) model or streaming detector requires a 6-class taxonomy:
$$\mathcal{C}_6 = \{\texttt{TRAI}, \texttt{PHAI}, \texttt{LEN}, \texttt{XUONG}, \texttt{UNKNOWN}, \texttt{SILENCE}\}$$

Without `UNKNOWN` (out-of-vocabulary non-target speech) and `SILENCE` (ambient background and non-speech audio), a 4-class classifier is forced to map every audio window into one of the four commands via softmax normalization ($\sum_{c} P(c) = 1$), causing catastrophic false triggers during streaming silence or non-command conversation.

This document establishes the rigorous engineering, acoustic, and governance specifications for generating the remaining two classes without compromising dataset integrity or introducing vocoder identity shortcuts.

## 2. Specification for the `UNKNOWN` Class

### 2.1 Vocabulary Selection & Phonetic Stratification
To prevent acoustic overfitting and ensure sharp decision boundaries, the `UNKNOWN` class must contain **250 balanced utterances** comprising three linguistic tiers:

1. **Acoustic Distractors / Near-Homophones (35%, ~88 clips):**
   Words that share initial consonants, vowel formants, or tonal inflections with the target keywords:
   - For **`TRAI`** (`/ʈaːj˧˥/`): `gái` (`/ɣaːj˧˥/`), `lái` (`/laːj˧˥/`), `chài` (`/t͡ɕaːj˨˩/`), `trời` (`/ʈəːj˨˩/`), `tròn` (`/ʈɔn˨˩/`), `tay` (`/taj˧˧/`).
   - For **`PHAI`** (`/faːj˧˩˨/`): `phòng` (`/fawŋ˨˩/`), `phần` (`/fən˨˩/`), `bài` (`/baːj˨˩/`), `hai` (`/haːj˧˧/`), `vải` (`/vaːj˧˩˨/`), `thoải` (`/tʰwaːj˧˩˨/`).
   - For **`LEN`** (`/len˧˧/`): `đen` (`/ɗɛn˧˧/`), `ken` (`/kɛn˧˧/`), `quen` (`/kwɛn˧˧/`), `luôn` (`/luən˧˧/`), `lăn` (`/lan˧˧/`), `lòng` (`/lawŋ˨˩/`).
   - For **`XUONG`** (`/suəŋ˧˥/`): `sương` (`/sɨəŋ˧˧/`), `buông` (`/buəŋ˧˧/`), `chuông` (`/t͡ɕuəŋ˧˧/`), `sườn` (`/sɨən˨˩/`), `xương` (`/sɨəŋ˧˧/`).

2. **Common Vietnamese Control & Navigation Verbs (35%, ~88 clips):**
   Monosyllabic and disyllabic conversational commands:
   - `dừng` (stop), `chạy` (run), `bật` (turn on), `tắt` (turn off), `mở` (open), `đóng` (close), `tiếp` (continue), `quay` (turn), `chờ` (wait), `nhanh` (fast), `chậm` (slow).

3. **General Conversational Words & Greeting Fillers (30%, ~74 clips):**
   - `xin` (please), `chào` (hello), `ơi` (hey), `vâng` (yes), `dạ` (polite yes), `không` (no), `người` (person), `nhà` (home), `xe` (vehicle), `đường` (street).

### 2.2 Strict Lexical Exclusion Policy
- **Prohibited Words:** Target keywords (`trái`, `phải`, `lên`, `xuống`) are strictly prohibited in isolation, in compound forms (`rẽ trái`, `đi lên`, `bên phải`), or embedded in phrases.
- **Automated Manifest Validator:** Every candidate text string must pass an exact normalized regex check rejecting any occurrence of target tokens.

### 2.3 Synthesizer Lineage & Avoidance of Vocoder Shortcuts
> [!CAUTION]
> **Vocoder Shortcut Vulnerability:** If `UNKNOWN` were generated using an external synthesizer (e.g. Edge TTS) or historical V5.2 additive buzzy synthesis while targets are generated by VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo, a neural network (e.g. TC-ResNet-14) will trivially achieve 100% training accuracy by detecting neural vocoder phase artifacts and bandwidth ceilings instead of learning phonetic representations.

- **Mandatory Synthesis Rule:** All 250 `UNKNOWN` utterances must be synthesized using the **exact same VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo engine** (`pnnbao-ump/VieNeu-TTS-v3-Turbo`) in `.venv_vieneu`.
- **Prohibition of V5.2 Audio:** Historical V5.2 parametric synthetic clips must **never** be substituted or mixed into the `UNKNOWN` partition.
- **Voice Preset Allocation:** The exact same 25 installed preset voices must be distributed across the 17 train, 4 val, and 4 test splits in the exact same proportions:
  - Train: 17 voices $\times$ 10 samples = 170 samples
  - Validation: 4 voices $\times$ 10 samples = 40 samples
  - Test: 4 voices $\times$ 10 samples = 40 samples
  - Total: 250 samples (zero voice leakage across splits).

## 3. Specification for the `SILENCE` Class

### 3.1 Taxonomy and Operational Definition
The `SILENCE` class must contain **250 balanced clips** (1.0000s, 16,000 samples at 16 kHz) capturing three acoustic regimes:

1. **Digital Silence & Low-Level Dither (20%, 50 clips):**
   - 25 clips of exact zeros ($x[n] = 0.0$).
   - 25 clips of uniform or triangular dither at $-90$ dBFS to $-80$ dBFS (RMS $< 10^{-4}$), representing inactive audio interfaces.

2. **Synthetic Room Tone & Stationary Acoustic Hum (50%, 125 clips):**
   - Low-level filtered Brownian / pink noise simulating indoor acoustic environments ($-60$ to $-45$ dBFS RMS).
   - Mains hum harmonics (50 Hz and 100/150 Hz overtones with $-50$ dBFS amplitude).
   - High-frequency HVAC / airflow noise filtered above 1 kHz.

3. **Non-Speech Acoustic Transients (30%, 75 clips):**
   - Synthetic transient clicks, finger snaps, microphone pops, and desk thumps (isolated impulse responses with peak amplitude between 0.05 and 0.40).
   - Non-periodic room rustles and synthetic dither bursts.

### 3.2 Leakage-Safe Noise Split Policy
- All noise seeds and parametric generation parameters must be strictly partitioned across splits:
  - Train: 170 clips (68.0%)
  - Validation: 40 clips (16.0%)
  - Test: 40 clips (16.0%)
- Continuous background streams must be chunked with **zero overlap** between splits to prevent background waveform leakage.

## 4. Dataset Harmony & Integrity Constraints

| Constraint | Requirement | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Window & Sample Rate** | Exactly 16,000 samples (1.0000s) at 16,000 Hz | Maintains bit-exact architectural contract with existing TC-ResNet feature extractors. |
| **Audio Format** | Dual-storage: 48 kHz raw master + 16 kHz KWS clip | Retains high-fidelity vocoder master archive. |
| **Headroom & Clipping** | Peak amplitude $\le 0.9500$; Zero clipping | Prevents digital distortion in acoustic feature extraction. |
| **Split Isolation** | Disjoint voice presets (17 train / 4 val / 4 test) | Prevents identity leakage across splits. |
| **Governance Invariant** | No natural human audio ingestion | Adheres to strict project governance blocking unapproved human recordings. |
