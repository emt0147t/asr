## 1. Executive Summary & Core Scientific Findings

Phase 12 conducts a scientific integrity audit of the Vietnamese Keyword Spotting (KWS) datasets, acoustic generators, model evaluation methodology, and generalization boundaries.

### Key Conclusions:

1. **High Holdout Accuracy is an Artifact of Shortcut Learning**:
   - The reported **99.33% accuracy** for candidate `arm_b_onset_no_resample.pt` on the V4 Fresh Holdout is **not representative of real-world acoustic performance**.
   - The V4 generator (`onset_aware_generator.py`) inadvertently introduced a deterministic, non-linguistic acoustic shortcut: `TRAI` was synthesized with a **25ms silence closure gap followed by a high-frequency noise burst (>3.5 kHz)**, while `PHAI` was synthesized with continuous frication starting at $t=0$, and `XUONG` with highpass noise.
   - When evaluated on speech generated by previous versions (V1, V2, V3) lacking this artificial gap, **Arm B's accuracy collapses from 99.33% to 69.44%**, with **0.0% recall on TRAI (100% of TRAI utterances misclassified as PHAI)**.
2. **Train/Test Partitions Are Acoustically Near-Identical**:
   - While speaker IDs and exact SHA-256 hashes are disjoint across Train, Validation, and Fresh Holdout splits, the synthetic generator uses **identical static formant trajectories** ($F_1=800$, $F_2=1350$, $F_3=2600$ Hz) and identical onset parameters across all speakers.
   - The average Log-Mel feature cosine similarity between the Training set and Fresh Holdout set is **0.966 to 0.986** (with maximum pairwise similarity reaching **0.9985**). The "fresh" holdout is an acoustic near-duplicate of the training distribution.
3. **Dead-Code Metadata**:
   - The `dialect` parameter (`"Northern"`, `"Central"`, `"Southern"`) in `generate_vietnamese_onset_aware_utterance` is **dead code** (never read or referenced in the function body). Audio records labeled as different dialects are acoustically identical in their synthesis formula.
4. **Action Adapter Masks Neural Classifier Failure on Silence**:
   - On pure zeros (`case_09`) and low-amplitude ambient noise (`case_10`), the neural network outputs **`TRAI` (30.23% confidence)** and **`UNKNOWN` (30.22%)**, with `SILENCE` receiving only **13.05%**.
   - Safe behavior (`NO_ACTION`) is achieved solely because `GameActionAdapter` applies a post-processing confidence threshold ($< 50\%$). The neural classifier itself has not learned the concept of digital silence.
5. **Retraining Decision**:
   - **Retraining on the current synthetic dataset is scientifically unjustified**, as it would simply relearn the artificial onset shortcuts.
   - **Retraining on human speech remains strictly blocked by governance**.
   - Retraining must be deferred until the acoustic generator is completely reformed with true phonetic diversity, randomized formants, real noise backgrounds, open-vocabulary negative mining, or until human data collection is authorized.

## 4. Generator Phonetics & Non-Linguistic Shortcut Analysis

Investigation of `experiments/synthetic_kws_v4/onset_aware_generator.py`:

### 1. The Glottal Vocal Source Model
```python
phase = 2 * np.pi * np.cumsum(f0) / sr
source = np.sin(phase) + 0.5 * np.sin(2 * phase) + 0.25 * np.sin(3 * phase) + 0.12 * np.sin(4 * phase)
```
- The glottal pulse is modeled as 4 static sine harmonics with fixed relative weights ($1.0, 0.5, 0.25, 0.12$).
- Real human vocal folds produce complex, time-varying airflow pulses with glottal closure moments, aspiration turbulence, jitter, and shimmer.

### 2. Formant Trajectories & Phonological Collapse
- Both `trái` and `phải` share **100% identical formant trajectories**:
  - $F_1(t) = 800 \times (1.0 - 0.4 \times t / T)$
  - $F_2(t) = 1350 \times (1.0 + 0.5 \times t / T)$
  - $F_3(t) = 2600$ Hz constant
- Because both words share the Vietnamese rhyme /-a:j/, the generator assumes identical vowel filters. Consequently, the steady-state vowel body provides almost no discriminative acoustic information between the two commands.

### 3. The Onset Shortcut (The "Smoking Gun")
To force the model to separate `trái` and `phải`, the V4 generator introduced:
- **`trái`**:
  - Lines 117-118: `output[:closure_samples] *= 0.05` ($25\text{ ms}$ closure gap).
  - Lines 121-127: Injected $15\text{ ms}$ high-pass filtered noise ($>3500\text{ Hz}$) with sharp exponential decay.
  - Line 163: Envelope starts at $0.05$ and stays suppressed until sample 400.
- **`phải`**:
  - Line 137: Zero closure gap.
  - Lines 137-144: Continuous $50\text{ ms}$ bandpass noise ($1800 - 6000\text{ Hz}$) starting at $t=0$.
- **`xuống`**:
  - Continuous $60\text{ ms}$ highpass noise ($>4000\text{ Hz}$).
- **`lên`**:
  - Zero noise injection; smooth vocal onset.

### 4. Dead-Code Dialect Parameter
- Line 48 accepts `dialect: str = "Northern"`.
- Across the entire 190 lines of `onset_aware_generator.py`, the variable `dialect` is **never referenced or branched on**.
- Utterances marked as "Central" or "Southern" in manifests receive the exact same Northern affricate burst and tone contour.

### 5. Narrow Negative Diversity
- `UNKNOWN` is synthesized using only **8 distinct words**: `chào`, `bạn`, `đi`, `xe`, `nhà`, `số`, `ngày`, `học`.
- `SILENCE` only contains synthetic Gaussian dither or lowpass-filtered noise. Digital zeros and common environmental noise (fan hum, keyboard taps, electrical hum) are completely absent.

## 7. Action Adapter vs Neural Classification

On numerical edge cases, the raw model outputs demonstrate that the neural network has not learned digital silence:

| Test Case | Intended Label | Model Prediction | Raw Model Confidence | Adapted Game Action | Classification Status |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| `case_09_silence_pure_zeros` | `SILENCE` | **`TRAI`** | 30.23% (Silence: 13.05%) | `NO_ACTION` | **FAILED** (Misclassified as TRAI) |
| `case_10_low_amplitude_ambient`| `SILENCE` | **`TRAI`** | 30.24% (Silence: 13.07%) | `NO_ACTION` | **FAILED** (Misclassified as TRAI) |
| `case_11_transient_impulse` | `UNKNOWN` | **`TRAI`** | 31.37% (Unknown: 20.49%) | `NO_ACTION` | **FAILED** (Misclassified as TRAI) |

- **Why the System Doesn't Break in Practice**: `GameActionAdapter` requires confidence $\ge 50.0\%$ to trigger movement. Because the model's confidence on silence is split ($\sim 30\%$ `TRAI`, $\sim 30\%$ `UNKNOWN`, $\sim 13\%$ `SILENCE`), the adapter suppresses it to `NO_ACTION`.
- **Scientific Implication**: A test that checks only `adapted_action == NO_ACTION` would falsely mark these cases as passing. A scientifically valid evaluation must evaluate **raw classifier logits and softmax probabilities**.

## 8. Retraining Decision & Evidence-Based Recommendations

### Decision: **DO NOT RETRAIN AT THIS TIME.**

1. **Why Retraining Now is Counterproductive**:
   - Training a new model on the existing V4 synthetic data will simply reproduce the same shortcut learning.
   - Retraining on human speech is **strictly blocked** by the governance invariant.
2. **Prerequisites for a Future Retraining Phase**:
   - **Overhaul the Synthetic Generator**:
     - Remove fixed 25ms silence gaps and artificial burst injections.
     - Implement continuous vocal tract length randomization ($\pm 15\%$) to randomize formants $F_1, F_2, F_3$ across speakers.
     - Implement true glottal pulse shaping with natural aspiration noise.
     - Implement actual dialect phonetics (e.g. Southern retroflex $/ʈ/$, diphthong variation).
     - Expand `UNKNOWN` vocabulary from 8 words to $>500$ open-vocabulary Vietnamese words from public corpora.
     - Include pure zeros, white noise, pink noise, electrical hum, and room impulse responses in `SILENCE`.
   - **Alternative Path**:
     - Await governance authorization for human speech data collection.
