## 1. RESEARCH QUESTIONS

1. **RQ1 (Generator Architecture Generalization):**  
   Does the frozen E1 KWS model (trained exclusively on `VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo`) generalize to audio produced by an unseen generator architecture (`VieNeu-TTS v3 Nano` operating with an independent ONNX-based vector estimator and codec decoder at 24 kHz)?

2. **RQ2 (Unseen Voice Distribution Robustness):**  
   Does E1 maintain accurate keyword recognition and rejection across genuinely novel synthetic voice presets (`Ái Hân`, `Hữu Quân`, `Xuân Tiên`, `Mạnh Dũng`) that never appeared in `dataset_v12` training, validation, or previous test sets?

3. **RQ3 (Principled Phonetic Confuser Discrimination):**  
   How effectively does E1 reject minimal phonetic pair OOV confusers (vowel shifts, tone contrasts, initial/coda consonant alterations such as `lên` vs `lăn`/`len`/`lân`, `phải` vs `phái`/`phay`/`phai`, `trái` vs `trại`/`trát`, `xuống` vs `xuồng`/`xưởng`) when generated by an independent synthesizer?

4. **RQ4 (End-to-End Streaming Performance Parity):**  
   When coupled with the frozen `SpeechBandRatioVAD` and `CanonicalLogMelFrontendNumPy` in Profile B, what are the empirical exact-sequence accuracy, command recall, command precision, false activation rates per minute, and latency distribution across calibrated inter-command gaps ($100\text{ ms}$ to $400\text{ ms}$) and acoustic SNR conditions ($0\text{ dB}$ to $\text{Clean}$)?

## 3. COMPONENT GENERATION & SEQUENCE ASSEMBLY CONTRACT

### 3.1 Synthesis & Preprocessing Parameters
- Native TTS generation rate: $24,000\text{ Hz}$ mono float32.
- Resampling: High-quality polyphase Fourier/Kaiser resampling to $16,000\text{ Hz}$.
- Normalization: Peak amplitude normalized to $0.85$, with leading/trailing energy trimming to measure ground-truth acoustic boundaries (energy threshold $-40\text{ dBFS}$).
- Clipping Prevention: Clamping strictly if $\max(|x|) > 1.0$; zero amplification below floor.

### 3.2 Sequence Assembly Protocol
- Each streaming sequence is constructed by concatenating target commands and OOV utterances separated by calibrated acoustic gaps:
  - Nominal gaps: $100\text{ ms}$, $150\text{ ms}$, $200\text{ ms}$, $250\text{ ms}$, $300\text{ ms}$, $400\text{ ms}$.
- Actual acoustic boundaries (onset and offset relative to sequence start in seconds) are recorded in `events_json`.
- Acoustic conditions:
  - `clean` (no added noise)
  - `snr20` ($20\text{ dB}$ calibrated SNR)
  - `snr15` ($15\text{ dB}$ calibrated SNR)
  - `snr10` ($10\text{ dB}$ calibrated SNR)
  - `snr05` ($5\text{ dB}$ calibrated SNR)
- All noise mixing uses calibrated RMS matching from isolated noise assets.

## 4. METRICS & ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

### 4.1 Evaluation Metrics
1. **Exact-Sequence Accuracy:** Percentage of sequences where emitted command sequence exactly equals the ground-truth sequence in order, count, and identity.
2. **Command Precision, Recall, and F1:** Evaluated at temporal intersection-over-union $\text{IoU} \ge 0.20$ or overlap $\ge 80\text{ ms}$.
3. **OOV False Activations:** Total target commands emitted during OOV speech segments.
4. **Silence False Activations:** Total target commands emitted during inter-command silence or environmental noise.
5. **False Activations per Minute:** Number of false triggers divided by total eligible negative audio duration in minutes.
6. **Trigger Latency:** Median (P50) and 95th-percentile (P95) latency from reference command acoustic onset to trigger emission.

### 4.2 Pre-Registered Acceptance Criteria
- **Pipeline Integrity:** Zero NaN/Inf outputs, 100% finite probabilities, 100% manifest-to-file hash matches.
- **Silence Robustness:** Silence false activations $\le 1$ event across the entire 40-sequence test corpus.
- **Duplicate Prevention:** Zero duplicate triggers ($0$ events).
- **Latency Upper Bound:** P95 latency $\le 650.0\text{ ms}$.
