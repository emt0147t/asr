## 1. Research Questions & Hypotheses

1. **RQ1 (Independent Generator Architecture Generalization):**  
   Does the frozen Phase 26 candidate (`D0_H1_seed44`), trained solely on VieNeu-TTS v3 Turbo, generalize to an independent neural TTS engine (`Microsoft Edge-TTS 7.3.1` using Microsoft Azure's transformer acoustic model and neural vocoder) without retraining?  
   *Hypothesis:* Candidate macro-F1 will exceed 80% on clean independent speech, but will experience moderate domain degradation relative to in-distribution validation.

2. **RQ2 (Independent Voice Distribution Robustness):**  
   How robust is the candidate model across two novel synthetic voices (`vi-VN-HoaiMyNeural` [Female] and `vi-VN-NamMinhNeural` [Male]) that have zero presence in historical training or validation sets?  
   *Hypothesis:* Female pitch characteristics may trigger higher false activations on certain phonetic confusers, while male speech will maintain higher precision.

3. **RQ3 (Out-of-Vocabulary Confuser Discrimination under Independent Prosody):**  
   Does the candidate reject minimal phonetic pairs and conversational OOV words generated by the independent engine, or does independent prosody/formant synthesis induce unexpected false triggers?  
   *Hypothesis:* $H_1$ hard-negative training will suppress OOV false alarms to $<30\%$ on independent speech, outperforming baseline v11 and E1.

4. **RQ4 (End-to-End Streaming Behavior and Latency):**  
   When evaluated in the locked Profile B streaming pipeline (`SpeechBandRatioVAD` + Slaney frontend + $\theta_{\text{cmd}} = 0.20$), what are the exact-sequence accuracy, event recall/precision, false activation rate per minute, and detection latency across 100 multi-command streaming sequences?

## 4. Test Corpus Construction Protocol

Two corpora are prepared:
1. **Isolated Component Corpus:**
   - 68 clean base utterances ($34 \text{ words} \times 2 \text{ voices}$).
   - 204 noise-augmented clips across 3 SNR tiers (20 dB, 15 dB, 10 dB) using held-out noise tracks (mic-noise tracks 34–39).
   - Total: 272 isolated 1.0s clips.
2. **Streaming Sequence Corpus (`edge_tts_eval_test_v1`):**
   - **Target Size:** Exactly **100 multi-command streaming sequences** (nominal duration 10–18 seconds each, total audio $\approx 25$ minutes).
   - **Event Composition per Sequence:** 3 to 6 speech events containing:
     - 2 to 4 target commands (including repeated commands and transitions).
     - 1 to 2 interspersed OOV words.
     - Calibrated silence/noise gaps between events: $200\text{ ms}$, $300\text{ ms}$, $500\text{ ms}$, $800\text{ ms}$, $1200\text{ ms}$.
   - **SNR Stratification:**
     - 25 Clean sequences
     - 25 SNR 20 dB sequences
     - 25 SNR 15 dB sequences
     - 25 SNR 10 dB sequences

## 5. Metric Definitions & Scoring Standards

1. **Four-Command True Macro-F1:**
   $$\operatorname{MacroF1} = \frac{1}{4} \sum_{c \in \{\text{TRAI, PHAI, LEN, XUONG}\}} F1_c$$
2. **REJECT Metrics:**
   - REJECT False Alarm Rate $= \frac{\text{False Alarms on REJECT}}{\text{Total REJECT Samples}}$
   - OOV False Alarm Rate $= \frac{\text{False Alarms on OOV}}{\text{Total OOV Samples}}$
   - Silence/Noise False Alarm Rate $= \frac{\text{False Alarms on Silence/Noise}}{\text{Total Silence/Noise Samples}}$
3. **Streaming Metrics:**
   - **Exact-Sequence Accuracy:** Percentage of streaming sequences with 0 insertion, 0 deletion, and 0 substitution errors.
   - **Event-Level Precision, Recall, F1:** Evaluated using a 500 ms acceptance window relative to ground-truth keyword onset.
   - **False Alarm Rate per Minute (FA/min):** Evaluated separately during active non-keyword speech (OOV speech time) and non-speech background (silence/noise time).
   - **Trigger Latency:** Median (P50) and 95th-percentile (P95) delay from ground-truth keyword onset to streaming trigger.
